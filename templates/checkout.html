How Your Transaction is Protected

AES-256-GCM Encryption

Order items and shipping details are encrypted using a fresh 256-bit key, 96-bit nonce, and 128-bit authentication tag.

RSA-OAEP Key Wrapping

The ephemeral AES-256 key is wrapped with the server's 3072-bit RSA public key using OAEP (SHA-256 & MGF1-SHA256).

RSA-PSS Digital Signature

The transaction envelope is digitally signed with your registered 3072-bit RSA key to guarantee authenticity and non-repudiation.

Replay & Price Protection

Atomic nonce consumption, UUIDv4 validation, ±300s UTC timestamp checks, and authoritative database price recomputation.
